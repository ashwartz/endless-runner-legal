# Privacy Policy — Endless Runner

**Effective date:** 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Avi Shwartz ("we," "us," "our") handles information in
the mobile game **Endless Runner** (the "App"), available on Google Play.

Endless Runner is built to be privacy-friendly and safe for all ages. **The game works fully
offline by default. We show no ads, use no analytics or tracking, and we never sell or
share your data.** The camera and the microphone are used only if **you choose** head control
or voice control, and what they capture is processed on your device and never sent anywhere.
The only time any information leaves your device is if **you choose** to see the optional
online leaderboard, which posts your best distances with the nickname and avatar you picked.

## 1. The short version
- The game works offline. Your records, coins, settings, calibration and recorded sounds are
  stored **on your device**.
- **Head control** uses the front camera to follow your head. The pictures are processed on your
  device, moment by moment, and are **never stored and never sent** to us or anyone else.
- **Voice control** uses the microphone to recognise four short sounds you record yourself. The
  recordings stay **on your device** and are never sent to us or anyone else.
- We collect **no personal information** unless you choose to open the public leaderboard. If you
  do, we store only a **nickname you choose, an avatar you pick, and your best distances** — no
  real name, email, phone number, or device location.
- You can have your leaderboard entry removed at any time.

## 2. Information stored on your device
The App saves your best distances, your coins, your settings (such as sound, the guide arrows
and the game's speed), your head calibration, the four sounds you record for voice control and,
to improve recognition, the microphone sound of your most recent voice runs (the newest few,
replaced as you play). All of this stays **locally on your device** and is never sent to us. You
can erase it by clearing the App's data, or by uninstalling the App.

## 3. The camera (head control, only if you choose it)
If you choose head control, the App uses the front camera to find your face and follow the way
your head moves, so that you can steer without touching the screen. The camera pictures are
analysed on your device as they come and are discarded at once. **No picture is stored, and no
picture or face data leaves your device.** The calibration screen shows you the camera picture
so that you can place your face; that picture is shown only to you. The App asks for the camera
permission the first time you choose head control, and you can withdraw it at any time in your
device settings.

## 4. The microphone (voice control, only if you choose it)
If you choose voice control, the App asks you to record four short sounds, one for each move,
and then listens for them while you play. The sounds are recognised on your device. The four
recordings, and the microphone sound of your most recent runs, are kept on your device only so
that recognition can improve; they are **never sent to us or to anyone else**, and they are
deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. The App asks for the microphone
permission the first time you choose voice control, and you can withdraw it at any time in your
device settings.

## 5. The optional online leaderboard (only if you choose to take part)
If you open the ALL PLAYERS board, the App sends and stores your row on the shared board:
- the **nickname** you chose in your profile (please choose a nickname, not your real name);
- the **avatar** you selected — a small cartoon picture chosen from a fixed set; it is not
  personal information;
- your **best distance** with each control method (touch, head and voice);
- an **anonymous ID** automatically created for your device, so your own entry can be updated
  or removed. This ID is not linked to your real identity; we do not ask you to create an
  account, sign in, or provide an email address.

This information is **publicly visible** on the in-app leaderboard to other players. Please do
not include personal information in your nickname.

**What we save, and what it's used for:**

| Data | Why we save it |
|---|---|
| Nickname (you choose) | Displayed on the public leaderboard |
| Avatar (you pick) | Displayed next to your nickname |
| Best distance per control method | To rank you on the leaderboard for that method |
| Anonymous user ID (auto-generated) | To identify, update, or delete only your own entry |

None of this is used for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

**How it's processed:** the leaderboard runs on **Google Firebase** (Cloud Firestore and
Anonymous Authentication, provided by Google LLC), acting as our service provider. Google
processes the data above — and standard technical data such as your IP address — to operate and
secure the service. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## 6. We do NOT
- Show advertising.
- Use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- Track you across other apps or websites.
- Store or send camera pictures, face data, or voice recordings.
- Collect your real name, email, phone number, contacts, device location, or files.
- Sell or rent your information.

## 7. Children's privacy
Endless Runner is suitable for children and may be used by them. We do **not knowingly collect
personal information from children**. The camera and the microphone are processed on the device
only. The optional leaderboard stores only a self-chosen nickname, a chosen avatar, and best
distances; nicknames are screened by a profanity filter, and we ask that they contain no personal
information. We may remove any entry or nickname that is inappropriate or contains personal
information. We comply with applicable children's privacy laws (including COPPA) and Google
Play's Families policy, and we never use any data for advertising or profiling. If you believe a
child has shared personal information, email aviappstore1@gmail.com and we will delete it.

## 8. How to delete your data
- **On-device data** (records, coins, settings, calibration, recorded sounds): clear the App's
  storage in your device settings, or uninstall the App.
- **Leaderboard entry:** email aviappstore1@gmail.com with the nickname shown on the board and we
  will delete it.

## 9. Data retention
On-device data remains until you remove it. A leaderboard entry is kept until you (or we) delete it.

## 10. Security
The leaderboard connection is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and we rely on Google Firebase's
infrastructure to store the limited data described above. No method of transmission or storage is
100% secure.

## 11. International users
If you use the leaderboard, the limited data is processed on Google's servers, which may be
located outside your country.

## 12. Changes to this policy
We may update this Policy. We will change the "Effective date" above and post the updated version
at this URL.
